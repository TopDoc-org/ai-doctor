Frequently asked questions

Is HealthGuide AI free?

Yes. HealthGuide AI by KnocDoc is 100% free to start. There is no sign-up and no credit card needed to describe your symptoms and get AI health guidance.

Is this an AI doctor or real medical advice?

HealthGuide AI is an AI health-information assistant, not a licensed physician. It gives educational information and suggests which specialist to see, but it does not provide a diagnosis, treatment, or prescription. In an emergency in India, call 112 or 108.

Which specialist should I see for my symptoms?

Describe what you're feeling in plain language and HealthGuide AI reads your concern and points you to the right speciality, then helps you find trusted doctors near you in India.

Do I need to sign up to use the AI symptom checker?

No. You can use the AI symptom checker instantly with no sign-up. You only create an account if you want to save your health summary or past consultations.

Can HealthGuide AI help me find a doctor near me in India?

Yes. After reviewing your symptoms, HealthGuide AI can help you find licensed doctors near you by city or location, with ratings, hours, and contact details.
