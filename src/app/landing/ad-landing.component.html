What {{ appName }} is

An information and navigation tool. It helps you understand which kind of practitioner fits your situation, gives you a written summary to carry to the consultation, and helps you find doctors nearby. It is built and operated by KnocDoc.

It is not a licensed physician and does not replace one. It does not provide medical advice, diagnosis, treatment, or prescriptions, and it cannot examine you or order tests. Use it to arrive at a consultation better prepared — never instead of one.

For adults 18 and over. Read the medical disclaimer, terms of use, and privacy policy.

Common questions

Is it really free?

Yes. There is no charge, no subscription, and no credit card. You can start without creating an account.

Do I have to create an account?

No. An account is only needed if you want to keep your written summary and come back to it later.

Which languages does it understand?

Type the way you talk — English, हिन्दी, Hinglish, or another language — and it replies in the same language you used.

Is this a consultation?

No. {{ appName }} is an information tool, not a consultation and not a telemedicine service. Whoever you decide to see is your own choice, booked directly with them.

What happens to what I type?

You are asked to agree before anything is processed, and you can read exactly what is kept and for how long in the privacy policy.
